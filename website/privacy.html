Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use RoBl, we collect the following information:

  • Roblox Account Information: When you log in via Roblox OAuth, we receive your Roblox User ID, display name, and avatar URL. This is used solely to authenticate you and display your profile.
  • AI Prompts and Generated Code: The prompts you enter and the code generated are stored temporarily to provide the service and allow the Roblox plugin to fetch your code.
  • NVIDIA NIM API Key: Your API key is stored in your browser's local storage. It is sent to our server only when generating code and is never stored on our server.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating your identity via Roblox OAuth
  • Sending your prompts to NVIDIA NIM for code generation
  • Delivering generated code to your Roblox Studio plugin
  • Improving the service through anonymous usage analytics

3. Data Storage and Retention

Session data is stored in memory and is cleared when the server restarts. Generated code is stored temporarily (keyed by your Roblox ID) and is deleted after being fetched by the plugin or after 24 hours. We do not permanently store your prompts or generated code.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share data with the following third parties only as necessary to provide the Service:

  • Roblox Corporation: Your User ID is sent to Roblox for OAuth authentication. This is governed by Roblox's Privacy Policy.
  • NVIDIA Corporation: Your prompts are sent to NVIDIA NIM for AI processing. This is governed by NVIDIA's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:

  • All API communication is encrypted
  • API keys are never stored on our server
  • Session tokens are randomly generated and stored securely
  • No permanent database storage of user data

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you (your Roblox ID and generated code)
  • Request deletion of your data by clearing your browser's local storage and restarting the server
  • Revoke RoBl's access to your Roblox account via your Roblox Account Settings

7. Children's Privacy

RoBl is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older, in compliance with Roblox' Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8. Cookies

RoBl uses local storage for session management. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies. Your session token is stored in localStorage and is not accessible to third parties.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
